Sudharshan Suresh

I'm a research scientist at Boston Dynamics, where I work on machine learning for the Atlas humanoid robot.

I earned my Ph.D. in Robotics from CMU, while working as a part-time researcher at FAIR (Meta) on the manipulation and tactile sensing team. My thesis enabled robots to learn from interaction using vision and touch.
